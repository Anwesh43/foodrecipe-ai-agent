Detailed Tandoori Recipes

Here are detailed, cook-ready tandoori-style recipes. Included: an authentic Tandoori Chicken pulled from a recipe database, plus two popular tandoori items (Paneer Tikka and Fish Tikka) as reliable, standard restaurant-style methods you can follow at home.

Tandoori Chicken

India Chicken Spicy
Source: BBC Good Food • Video: YouTube

Ingredients

  • 2 lemons (juice)
  • 4 tsp paprika
  • 2 red onions, finely chopped
  • 16 skinless chicken thighs
  • Vegetable oil (for brushing)
  • 300 ml Greek yogurt
  • 1 large piece ginger
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • ¾ tsp garam masala
  • ¾ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • ¼ tsp turmeric

Method

  1. Pre-season: Mix lemon juice with paprika and red onions in a shallow dish. Slash each chicken thigh 3 times. Coat in the mixture and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Marinate: Combine yogurt, ginger, garlic, garam masala, cumin, chilli powder and turmeric. Pour over chicken, mix well, cover and chill at least 1 hour (up to 24 hours).
  3. Cook: Heat grill (or oven broiler). Place chicken on a rack over a tray. Brush lightly with oil and grill ~8 minutes per side, until lightly charred and fully cooked through.
Tip: For stronger tandoor-style char, cook on high heat and finish with a quick blast under the broiler. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

Tandoori Paneer Tikka

Vegetarian Grill / Oven / Air Fryer Restaurant-style
Serves 3–4 • Marinate 30–120 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g paneer, 1.5-inch cubes
  • 1 bell pepper (capsicum), squares
  • 1 large onion, squares/petals
  • Marinade: ½ cup thick yogurt (hung/Greek)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1½ tbsp besan (gram flour), lightly roasted (optional but helps coating)
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder (color + mild heat)
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (or any oil) + a pinch of smoked paprika/coal smoke optional
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Prep: Pat paneer dry. Cut onion and capsicum into similar-sized pieces.
  2. Marinate: Whisk marinade ingredients until smooth. Gently coat paneer + veggies. Rest 30 minutes (up to 2 hours refrigerated).
  3. Skewer: Thread onion–paneer–capsicum alternating. Brush lightly with oil.
  4. Cook:
    • Oven: 220°C / 425°F, 12–15 min, then broil 2–3 min for char.
    • Air fryer: 200°C, 8–10 min, shake/turn halfway.
    • Pan: Grill pan on high, cook 2–3 min per side until browned.
  5. Serve: Sprinkle chaat masala + lemon. Serve with mint chutney and onions.
Tip: Use thick yogurt; watery yogurt makes marinade runny and prevents browning.

Tandoori Fish Tikka

Seafood Quick cook High heat
Best fish: salmon, mahi-mahi, cod, tilapia (firm fillets)

Ingredients

  • 600 g fish fillets, 1.5-inch cubes
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Marinade: ½ cup thick yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp oil (mustard oil if you like)
  • 1 tbsp roasted besan or rice flour (optional for crisp edges)
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Pre-season: Toss fish with lemon juice + salt. Rest 10 minutes.
  2. Marinate: Mix marinade ingredients; coat fish gently. Marinate 20–40 minutes (avoid very long marination; fish can get mushy).
  3. Cook (choose one):
    • Oven/broiler: 220°C for 10–12 minutes, then broil 1–2 minutes for char.
    • Air fryer: 200°C for 7–9 minutes depending on thickness.
    • Skillet/grill pan: High heat, 2–3 minutes per side.
  4. Serve: Mint-coriander chutney, lemon wedges, sliced onions; optional sprinkle of chaat masala.
Tip: Keep pieces uniform and cook hot/fast. Fish dries out if overcooked—pull it as soon as it flakes.
Classic tandoori spice profile: Kashmiri chilli, cumin, coriander, garam masala, turmeric + ginger/garlic + lemon.
For smoky “tandoor” flavor (home trick): After cooking, place a small steel cup with hot charcoal in the center, add a tsp ghee, cover 2 min (dhungar).
