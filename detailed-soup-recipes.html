Creamy Tomato Soup

British • Starter
Smooth & creamy Make-ahead friendly Freezer: up to 3 months
YouTube Source

Ingredients

  • Olive Oil — 3 tbsp
  • Onions — 2 chopped
  • Celery — 2 sticks
  • Carrots — 300g
  • Potatoes — 500g
  • Bay Leaf — 4
  • Tomato Puree — 5 tblsp
  • Sugar — 2 tblsp
  • White Vinegar — 2 tblsp
  • Chopped Tomatoes — 1½ kg
  • Passata — 500g
  • Vegetable Stock Cube — 3
  • Whole Milk — 400ml

Steps (condensed)

  1. Gently fry oil + onions, celery, carrots, potatoes, bay leaves until onions soften (10–15 min).
  2. Stir in tomato purée, sugar, vinegar, chopped tomatoes, passata; crumble in stock cubes.
  3. Add 1 litre boiling water; simmer covered ~15 min until potato is tender; remove bay leaves.
  4. Blend until very smooth; season and adjust sweetness if needed.
  5. Reheat gently and stir in milk (avoid boiling). Serve.

Rosół (Polish Chicken Soup)

Polish • Chicken
Clear broth Comfort classic Great with noodles
YouTube Source

Ingredients

  • Chicken Legs — 4 (chopped)
  • Onions — 2 large
  • Carrots — 5 (chopped)
  • Leek — 1 (chopped)
  • Celery — 1 small
  • Cabbage — 1/4
  • Cloves — 1 whole
  • Allspice — 1 tsp
  • Bay Leaf — 2
  • Parsley — 4 sprigs
  • Dill — 4 sprigs
  • Pepper — 1 tsp
  • Salt — 1 tbsp

Steps (condensed)

  1. Add chicken to a stock pot, cover with water, bring to a boil; simmer 2–2.5 hours, skimming for a clear broth.
  2. Add vegetables + spices for the last hour (or cook separately if pot is small).
  3. Strain broth; pick chicken meat from bones; slice carrots.
  4. Return chicken and carrots to broth; season as needed.
  5. Cook noodles separately; add to bowl and ladle soup over.

French Onion Soup

French • Side
Caramelized onions Cheesy toast topping Dinner-party friendly
YouTube Source

Ingredients

  • Butter — 50g
  • Olive Oil — 1 tbsp
  • Onion — 1 kg
  • Sugar — 1 tsp
  • Garlic Clove — 4 (sliced)
  • Plain Flour — 2 tbsp
  • Dry White Wine — 250ml
  • Beef Stock — 1L
  • Bread — 4 slices
  • Gruyère — 140g

Steps (condensed)

  1. Melt butter + oil; cook onions covered 10 min until soft.
  2. Add sugar; cook 20 min more, stirring, until deeply caramelized; add garlic last few minutes.
  3. Stir in flour; gradually add wine then hot stock. Simmer covered 15–20 min.
  4. Toast bread; ladle soup into bowls; top with toast + Gruyère; grill until melted.

Black Bean Soup

Cuban-inspired • Starter
15–20 min simmer Blend to your texture Toppings make it pop
Source

Ingredients

  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil — 2 tbsp
  • Red Onions — 1 medium
  • Jalapeno — 1
  • Garlic — 2 cloves (minced)
  • Tomato Puree — 1 tbsp
  • Kosher Salt — pinch
  • Black Pepper — pinch
  • Ground Cumin — 1/2 tsp
  • Chilli Powder — 1 tsp
  • Black Beans — 3 cans
  • Chicken Stock — 3 cups
  • Bay Leaf — 1
  • Sour Cream — garnish
  • Avocado — garnish
  • Cilantro — garnish
  • Lime — to serve

Steps (condensed)

  1. Sauté onion in oil until soft (~5 min). Add jalapeño + garlic; cook 2 min.
  2. Stir in tomato paste; season with salt, pepper, cumin, chili powder.
  3. Add beans + broth + bay leaf; bring to boil, then simmer 15–20 min to thicken (add broth to thin).
  4. Remove bay leaf; blend partially or fully to desired texture.
  5. Serve with sour cream, avocado, cilantro, and lime.
