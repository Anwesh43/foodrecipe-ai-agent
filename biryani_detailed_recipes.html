Lamb Biryani

India Category: Lamb Tags: Curry, Meat
Restaurant-style layered biryani: rich lamb masala + 80% cooked basmati + dum finish.

Ingredients (with measures)

  • Cashew nuts — 12
  • Khus khus (poppy seeds) — ½ tbsp
  • Cumin seeds — ½ tbsp
  • Onions — 3, sliced thinly
  • Ginger garlic paste — 2 tsp
  • Garlic — 4 whole
  • Mint leaves — Leaves
  • Cilantro (coriander leaves) — Leaves
  • Saffron — ½ tsp dissolved in ½ cup warm milk
  • Ghee — 2 tbsp
  • Basmati rice — 2 cups
  • Full fat yogurt — ½ cup
  • Cumin seeds (jeera) — 1 tbsp
  • Bay leaf — ½
  • Cinnamon — 1 thin piece
  • Cloves — 3
  • Cardamom — 2
  • Lamb — 1 lb
  • Red chilli powder — 1 tsp
  • Biryani masala — 1 tbsp
Pro tip: For fluffier rice, rinse until water runs mostly clear and soak for 20 min. Use aged basmati if possible.

Method (step-by-step)

1) Grind cashew + poppy seeds + cumin into a smooth paste with minimal water. 2) Deep-fry sliced onions in hot oil (don’t overcrowd). Drain on paper towel until crisp. Fry cashews till golden; set aside. 3) Wash rice and soak for 20 minutes. 4) In a wide pan: heat oil (medium). Add sliced onions, then the ground paste, green chillies, ginger-garlic paste, and garlic; fry 1 minute. 5) Add tomatoes; sauté until cooked but not mushy. 6) Add red chilli powder, biryani powder/masala, mint, coriander; sauté well. 7) Add yogurt (move pan off heat to prevent curdling), mix, return to heat. 8) Add lamb + salt + ½ cup water. Cook covered on medium-low about 1 hour, or pressure cook ~6 whistles. Reduce excess liquid if needed. 9) In another pot: boil water (about 3× the rice volume). Add rice + salt + jeera; cook until ~80% done (~7 min). Drain. 10) Layer: level lamb masala, top with hot drained rice. Garnish with fried onions, ghee, mint, coriander, and saffron milk. 11) Dum finish: bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 min covered, OR stovetop 12 min medium + 5 min low. Rest, then mix and serve hot.
Note
If it turns too spicy, squeeze lemon on top—reduces heat and brightens flavor. Source: Relish The Bite • Video: YouTube
